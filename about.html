About Salary Ledger

Salary Ledger is a free online salary and wage calculator designed to make pay conversions simple and easy to understand.

What does Salary Ledger do?

The calculator allows users to convert between annual, monthly, daily and hourly gross pay. Users can also adjust the assumed number of working hours, days per week and weeks per year.

Our goal

Our goal is to provide a simple, fast and accessible tool that helps people understand different ways of expressing salary and hourly wages.

How the calculations work

The calculator uses the work pattern entered by the user. The default assumption is 8 hours per day, 5 days per week and 52 weeks per year.

Salary Ledger does not provide tax, legal or financial advice. The results are general calculations and actual income may vary depending on employment conditions, taxes and other deductions.

Privacy

The salary calculations are performed directly in the user's browser. Salary figures entered into the calculator are not intentionally sent to or stored by Salary Ledger.
