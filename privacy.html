Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Salary Ledger handles information when you use our website and salary calculator.

1. Information We Collect

Salary Ledger does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the salary calculator.

Salary figures entered into the calculator are processed directly in your web browser. We do not intentionally collect or store the salary figures you enter into the calculator.

2. How the Calculator Works

The calculator performs salary and wage calculations directly on your device. The information entered into the calculator is used to calculate annual, monthly, daily and hourly values.

Salary Ledger may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of external websites you visit.

7. Children's Privacy

Salary Ledger is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.
