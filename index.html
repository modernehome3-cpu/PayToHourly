How the conversion works

Every figure on the ledger comes from the same three numbers: how many hours you work in a day, how many days in a week, and how many weeks in a year. Change any one pay figure and the calculator solves backward through the same relationship to update the rest.

annual = hourly × hours/day × days/week × weeks/year
monthly = annual ÷ 12
daily = hourly × hours/day

The default assumption — 8 hours a day, 5 days a week, 52 weeks a year — reflects a typical full-time schedule in both the US and Europe. If you get paid holiday or take unpaid leave, adjust the weeks-per-year field to match your actual working weeks for a more accurate hourly rate.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an hourly wage to an annual salary?

Multiply your hourly rate by hours per day, then by days per week, then by weeks per year. With the standard assumptions that's hourly × 8 × 5 × 52, or hourly × 2,080.

How is monthly salary calculated from annual salary?

Divide the annual figure by 12. Salaried pay is normally spread evenly across the year, so this holds regardless of how many days fall in a given month.

Does this calculator account for taxes?

No. It converts gross pay between time periods only. Income tax, social contributions and take-home pay vary too much by country, state and personal circumstances to include here.

Why does the daily rate not match annual ÷ 365?

Daily pay here means pay for one working day, not one calendar day. It's calculated as hourly rate × hours per day, so weekends and non-working days are excluded.
